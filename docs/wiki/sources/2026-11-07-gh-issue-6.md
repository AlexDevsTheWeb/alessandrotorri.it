# Summary

Issue #6 tracks the majors deferred from the in-range refresh. Each one is evaluated on its own with lint, build and a browser check. MUI 9 was adopted earlier ([decision 0006](/decisions/0006-adopt-mui-9.md)).

# Analysis

- Vite 8 + `@vitejs/plugin-react` 6, ESLint 10 + `@eslint/js` 10 + `eslint-plugin-react-refresh` 0.5, `uuid` 14 and `globals` 17 build and lint green with no config changes.
